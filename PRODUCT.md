# Product

## Register

product

## Users

**B2B customers** — contractors sourcing adhesives for jobs, procurement officers comparing specs and submitting quote requests, resellers and OEM customers evaluating bulk or custom solutions. They visit the site with a specific adhesive need, want to find the right product fast, and submit an enquiry. They are not browsing for pleasure.

**Yee Lim admin staff** — employees who manage the product catalogue, view enquiries, and update availability. They use the admin panel daily and need clarity and efficiency over decoration.

## Product Purpose

Transform Yee Lim Adhesives Industries' outdated website into a modern, trustworthy digital platform that helps B2B customers find the right adhesive product quickly and submit an enquiry with minimal friction. The admin panel lets Yee Lim staff manage their catalogue and respond to enquiries without developer help.

Success: a procurement officer lands on the products page, filters by industry and surface, finds the right product, adds it to their enquiry basket, and submits a quote request — in under two minutes.

## Brand Personality

Established. Precise. Dependable.

The brand has been operating for 50+ years in industrial Singapore. It is not trying to look modern for modernity's sake. It should feel like a trusted supplier — authoritative, clear, without fuss. The red (#CC2929) is the only accent. Everything else earns its place.

## Anti-references

- Consumer e-commerce (Shopify stores, Amazon-style layouts — this is B2B, not retail)
- Typical SaaS/startup look (gradients, glassmorphism, purple/blue hero sections)
- Generic "professional" corporate sites with stock photography and hollow taglines
- Flashy animations that distract from product finding
- Dashboard-template admin panels that look like off-the-shelf Bootstrap themes

## Design Principles

1. **Product first, decoration second.** Every design decision must make products easier to find or enquire about. If a visual element does not serve that goal, remove it.
2. **Density earns trust.** B2B buyers expect useful information, not whitespace for its own sake. Show specs, industries, surfaces, and features without hiding them behind clicks.
3. **The red is earned.** #CC2929 is used only for actions, active states, and the brand mark. It must never lose its signal value through overuse.
4. **Admin is a tool, not a showpiece.** The admin panel should feel like Linear or Stripe's dashboard — fast, clear, no decoration. Staff who use it daily should feel efficient, not impressed.
5. **Clarity over cleverness.** Labels, headings, and CTAs say exactly what they mean. No marketing speak in functional interfaces.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA minimum. Sufficient colour contrast on all text. Keyboard navigable throughout. Focus indicators visible. Form errors clearly communicated inline.
